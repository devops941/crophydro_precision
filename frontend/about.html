The Science of Crop Water Precision

Crop Hydro-Precision strictly implements the globally recognized FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56 standards to guide sustainable water use.

1. Reference Evapotranspiration (ET₀)

Reference evapotranspiration represents the rate of water loss from a standardized well-watered reference grass surface under given weather conditions. We use the Hargreaves-Samani method (1985), which calculates ET₀ using daily temperature extrema and extraterrestrial radiation (Ra), making it ideal for locations without solar radiation sensors.

FAO-56 Mathematical Equations:

dr = 1 + 0.033 × cos(2πJ / 365)
δ = 0.409 × sin(2πJ / 365 − 1.39)
ωs = arccos(−tan φ × tan δ)
Ra = (24 × 60 / π) × Gsc × dr × [ωs sin φ sin δ + cos φ cos δ sin ωs] (MJ/m²/day)
Ra (mm/day) = 0.408 × Ra

ET₀ = 0.0023 × Ra_mm × (Tmean + 17.8) × √(Tmax − Tmin)

2. The Crop Coefficient (Kc)

The crop coefficient integrates crop characteristics and average effects of soil evaporation. In FAO-56, the seasonal growth cycle is partitioned into four distinct phases: Initial, Crop Development, Mid-Season, and Late Season. Crop Hydro-Precision uses piecewise linear interpolation to determine the exact daily Kc value.

4-Stage Growth Boundaries:
  • Initial: Constant Kc = Kc_ini until end of initial days.
  • Development: Linear increase from Kc_ini to Kc_mid: Kc = Kc_ini + [(day − end_ini) / dev_days] × (Kc_mid − Kc_ini)
  • Mid-Season: Plateau at maximum canopy cover: Kc = Kc_mid
  • Late-Season: Linear decline toward maturity: Kc = Kc_mid + [(day − end_mid) / late_days] × (Kc_end − Kc_mid)

3. Crop Evapotranspiration (ETc)

Crop water demand is calculated as ETc = Kc × ET₀. This represents the actual depth of water in millimeters transpired by the crop and evaporated from the soil surface.

ETc = Kc × ET₀ (mm/day)

4. Net & Gross Irrigation Requirement

Accounting for natural rainfall, Effective Rainfall is determined as 80% of total precipitation. Net irrigation requirement is the net water depth needed: Net = max(ETc - Effective Rain, 0). Gross irrigation accounts for system distribution efficiency (e.g. 90% for Drip, 75% for Sprinkler, 60% for Surface Flood).

Effective Rain = 0.8 × Rain_mm
Net Irrigation = max(ETc − Effective Rain, 0)
Gross Irrigation = Net Irrigation / Efficiency

5. Volumetric & Pump Operation Schedule

A water depth of 1 mm applied over an area of 1 square meter equals exactly 1 litre of water. Using the farmer's pump discharge capacity (L/h), the required pump operating duration is determined with mathematical precision.

Litres = Gross_mm × Area_m²
m³ = Litres / 1000
Weekly Litres = Litres × 7
Pump Hours = Litres / Pump_Capacity (L/h)

Primary Scientific Citation

Allen, R. G., Pereira, L. S., Raes, D., & Smith, M. (1998). Crop evapotranspiration: Guidelines for computing crop water requirements. FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper No. 56. Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, Rome, Italy.
