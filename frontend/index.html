How Precision Calculation Works

Four scientific steps based on FAO-56 equations to calculate exact crop water needs.

1

Weather Inputs

Enter daily maximum/minimum temperature, rainfall, and latitude to determine atmospheric evaporative demand.

2

Reference ET₀

The Hargreaves-Samani equation computes extraterrestrial radiation (Ra) and reference evapotranspiration.

3

Crop Coefficient (Kc)

Dynamic piecewise interpolation calculates exact Kc according to the crop's precise growth stage day.

4

Irrigation Schedule

Accounting for irrigation efficiency and effective rain delivers net mm, gross volume in litres, and pump run hours.
